# References

## GPEDC

The canonical reference for the indicator definitions and means of verification is the **GPEDC 2023-26 Methodological Note** and its annexes (1.1 Monitoring Questionnaire, 6 Guidance for Development Partners, 7 Guidance for CSOs, 8 Guidance for Trade Unions, 9 Guidance for Private Sector Actors, 2 KPA Guidance, 3 Glossary). All are available from the GPEDC at:

- [effectivecooperation.org — Monitoring](https://www.effectivecooperation.org/landing-page/monitoring)

## OECD-DAC

Public documents of the DAC Working Party on Development Finance Statistics (WP-STAT) are referenced by document number; the canonical source is the OECD's public document register:

- **DCD/DAC/STAT(2026)22** — *Linking IATI and CRS: A Multi-Layer Matching Analysis (2000–2024).* The four-layer matching pipeline (A1/A2 → A9 → identifier-parsing → TF-IDF) referenced throughout the [CRS × IATI interoperability](methodology/crs-iati-interoperability.md) page.
- **DCD/DAC/STAT(2025)56** — *Exploring methods to track aid subcontracts.* The IATI Tier-2 supplement pilot referenced on the [Untying & subcontracts](methodology/untying-subcontracts.md) page.

Both are available via the OECD document portal at [one.oecd.org](https://one.oecd.org/) (search by document number).
